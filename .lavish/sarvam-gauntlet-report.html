  • All 20 date-form conversations (कल/परसों/weekday/two-weeks/absolute × 4 languages) - the calendar+tool fix is bulletproof

Where the 19 misses cluster two patterns

  • Update-type transactions in Marwari/English (13 of 19): "पइसा आयग्या सा" / "दे दियो सा" / "is now due Monday" often produced a polite reply but no tool call - the model acts readily on add verbs but under-triggers on update verbs outside Hindi. In Hindi/Hinglish the same transactions pass.
  • Quarter-fraction number words (4 of 19): सवा सौ (₹125) became ₹150 twice; "one fifty" once became ₹1500. साढ़े/डेढ़/ढाई are solid; सवा is not.

Implication for the decision: if mom's Marwari leans into updates ("दे दियो सा, पइसा आयग्या") rather than just orders, today's Sarvam config would miss roughly half of those actions - she'd get a warm reply and an unchanged ledger. That specific risk is where Claude's judgment premium still lives. Total spend for all 638 calls: ₹0 on the promo.

What's still not fixed - the honest residue (post-mega-round)

1. Update-verbs in Marwari & English top risk - 13 of 19 mega misses

Payments, deliveries, and reschedules phrased as "पइसा आयग्या सा" / "दे दियो सा" / "is now due Monday" often get a warm, correct-sounding reply with no tool call behind it - the ledger silently unchanged. The same transactions in Hindi/Hinglish pass. This is the most dangerous failure shape a ledger can have: mom hears "जी, ठीक है सा", the बही doesn't move. Guardable next (explicit update-verb trigger lines per register), unguarded today.

2. Disambiguation instinct real gap

With two clients named सुनीता, it never asks "कौन सी?" upfront at any effort - it proposes the order first. At low effort the read-back flow still landed on the right client; at medium it quietly created a brand-new client "वर्मा" - exactly the ledger pollution the design forbids. Judgment, not knowledge - the class of thing Claude gets right by instinct.

3. सवा - quarter fractions 4 of 19 mega misses

सवा सौ (₹125) became ₹150 twice; "one fifty" once became ₹1500. साढ़े, डेढ़, and ढाई are reliable across hundreds of checks - सवा specifically is not. A one-line prompt rule with examples is the likely fix; until then the read-back confirmation is the safety net (mom hears the wrong number before it's written).

4. High-effort weirdness avoidable by policy

High effort re-broke scenarios, stalled to 13.4s worst-case, and drifted garment spellings. The operational rule is settled: run this model at LOW effort - also the cheapest and fastest setting. Its extra thinking does not convert into extra correctness on this workload.
